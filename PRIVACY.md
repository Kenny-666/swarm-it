# Privacy policy: Draftline

_Last updated: November 5, 2026 (version 1.0.0)_

Draftline is a Chrome extension that draws the current web page as an animated blueprint. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **It only runs when you ask.** Draftline uses Chrome's `activeTab` permission, so it can access only the tab where you click its toolbar button or press its shortcut, and only at that moment. It has no access to your other tabs, your browsing history or any website you haven't started it on.
- **Everything happens on your device.** To draw the blueprint, Draftline reads the positions and sizes of elements on the page in your browser. That information is used only for the animation, stays in that tab's memory, and is discarded when the drawing ends or you leave the page.
- **No network requests.** Draftline doesn't contact any server, doesn't use analytics or tracking, and doesn't load remote code.
- **No storage.** Nothing is saved to cookies, `localStorage`, `chrome.storage` or anywhere else.
- **No accounts, no ads.**

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
| --- | --- |
| `activeTab` | Run on the tab you started Draftline on, and only that tab. |
| `scripting` | Add Draftline's bundled drawing script to that tab. |
