Support

Need help with Swarmline? Most answers are below. Still stuck? Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or open an issue on GitHub.

Getting started

  1. Install Swarmline from the Chrome Web Store, then pin it from the puzzle-piece menu next to the address bar.
  2. Open any website and click the Swarmline icon (or press Alt + Shift + L).
  3. Watch, tour, go live: Touring is on by default and T toggles it, M turns sound on or off, Go live fades back to the real page when it's built, H hides or shows the controls, Esc exits. Drag the control bar to move it; double-click it to put it back.

Common questions

The icon shows a red × and nothing happens

Chrome doesn't let any extension run on its own pages (chrome:// pages, the Chrome Web Store) or inside the built-in PDF viewer. Open a normal website and try again.

How do I record a video of it?

Use any screen recorder (Windows: Win + Alt + R, or the Snipping Tool's record mode; macOS: Shift + Cmd + 5). Start Swarmline (Touring is already on), press H to hide the controls (or drag them to a corner), and pick 2× for a snappy reel or 1× for a calm walkthrough. Press H again to bring the controls back.

Can I click the page while it's being built?

Not while it's building: the rebuild plays on a canvas over the page. You can still scroll (turn Touring off with T first), and the swarm keeps building what you scroll to. When it's done, press Go live and the real page fades back, fully interactive. Esc exits at any time.

Does it change or save the website?

No. Swarmline reads the page in your own browser tab and plays the rebuild over it. Nothing is saved, nothing is sent anywhere, and when you go live or exit, the page is exactly as it was. Reloading the page also clears it completely.

Some part of a page didn't animate, it just appeared

Very small details are placed together with the shape they sit in, and on very long pages the rebuild stops at a length limit; everything else appears when you press Go live.

The shortcut doesn't work

Another extension may already use Alt + Shift + L. Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts and set a different one for Swarmline.

Can I make it calmer or faster?

Use the 1× / 2× / 3× button in the toolbar. If your system is set to reduce motion, Swarmline builds at the fastest speed so the animation is short.

Report a problem

Please include the website address, your Chrome version (chrome://version) and what you saw. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or open an issue.
